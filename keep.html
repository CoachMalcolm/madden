Keeping it running

Submissions, the public lists and this website run by themselves. Nobody has to approve anything. They only need two accounts to stay alive.

Updated October 24, 2026 · User Guide · Lists

WHAT RUNS BY ITSELF

Every hour, automatically

  • The Madden Submissions script (a separate script in Malcolm's Google account) reads the submit form.
  • Players sent to the Master are checked (name, position, Clone check OK, house rules, no duplicates) and added.
  • Lists sent to be public are checked (22+ players, title, description, house rules, no broken players), copied into the Public Madden Lists folder and listed on the lists page.
  • Reports are counted. Three reports from different people take a list down.
  • Everyone gets an email with the result.
ONCE · GOOGLE

Google: Inactive Account Manager

Google can delete an account that isn't used for 2 years, which would stop everything. Inactive Account Manager lets you choose people who get access to your Drive (the workbooks, the folder and the script) if the account goes quiet.

Set it up at myaccount.google.com/inactive.

ONCE · GITHUB

GitHub: account successor

This website and the Roster Kit live in the CoachMalcolm/madden repository. GitHub lets you name a successor who can take over your repositories.

Set it up in GitHub: Settings › Account › Successor settings (github.com/settings/admin).
