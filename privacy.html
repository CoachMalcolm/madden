Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy covers the Madden Player List Google Sheets workbook and its Apps Script macros ("the app").

What the app accesses

When you authorize the app, Google lets it read and edit the spreadsheet it is attached to, and identify which Google account is running it. The app uses this only to run its macros: sorting lists, checking entries against the workbook's rules, and recording submissions in the workbook.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or send your data anywhere outside the spreadsheet and your Google account.
  • It does not sell, share or rent any information to anyone.
  • It does not use your data for advertising.
  • It does not access your Gmail, Drive files, contacts or any other spreadsheets.

Data in the spreadsheet

Anything you type into the workbook stays in the workbook, under Google's own storage and privacy terms. People the workbook is shared with can see what's in it, as with any shared Google Sheet.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
