Madison Madden 08 · User Guide

Put your people in Madden 08

Short steps, one at a time. Each step has a Learn more line if you want the detail. It opens right here, and Hide folds it away. Nothing opens a new window.

For the Master Madden List workbook and the Madden Roster Kit · Updated October 23, 2026

START HERE

What you can do

Add people you played with or against. Give each one the ratings of an NFL great. Put them on squads. Build a roster file and play them in Madden NFL 08 on your PC.

Learn more: how the pieces fit
  1. The workbook (a Google Sheet) holds your people and squads.
  2. The Roster Kit (a folder on your PC) turns the workbook into a roster file.
  3. Madden 08 loads that roster file.

Each person is a clone: an exact copy of one NFL player's ratings from one season, with your person's name and number on it.

STEP 1 · ONE TIME

Get set up

  1. Make your own copy of the workbook: open the Master Madden List and click Make a copy. It lands in your own Google Drive.
  2. Unzip the Madden Roster Kit to C:\MaddenRosterKit.
Learn more: what the PC needs
  • Madden NFL 08 for PC, with the same roster mod the kit was made for (the FF mod). A different mod can freeze the game at kickoff.
  • Python 3 from python.org. When you install it, tick Add python.exe to PATH. (A version that needs no install is on the way.)
Learn more: the locks

Most of the workbook is locked so nothing breaks by accident. You can type only in the open cells. If something won't let you type, it's meant to be filled in automatically.

Tip for the owner: do everyday editing from a second Google account with Editor access. The owner's account skips every lock.

STEP 2 · PLAYERS TAB

Add a player

  1. Type the first and last name (columns B and C).
  2. Pick the Position (F). Do this before the clone.
  3. Pick the Clone (I): click the arrow and type part of a name.
  4. Check Clone check (J). OK means he's ready.
  5. Pick where he made his bones (H) and his Skin tone (N).
Learn more: every column
ColWhat goes there
B, CFirst name (10 letters max), last name (12 max). Letters, space, period, apostrophe, hyphen.
DThe season his ratings are based on. 4 digits, or blank.
EJersey number 0–99. Blank = the clone's number.
FPosition. The clone list only shows players at this position.
GNotes and nicknames. Nothing here changes the game.
HWhere he made his bones. This colors his row.
I, JThe clone, and the automatic check.
K, LWho picked the clone, and where the info came from. Notes only.
MHis squads (Step 4).
N, OSkin tone (Light, Medium, Dark) and affiliation.
Learn more: finding the right clone

Use Madden Tools › Find a clone. A panel opens on the right. Search by name, team, season, number or overall, or by two ratings such as Speed and Kick return. Click Use to put that clone on the row you've selected.

If the Clone check says MISMATCH, the clone plays a different position than the one in F. Change one of them.

Learn more: facts only

If you don't know something, leave it blank or write Unknown. Don't guess.

OPTIONAL · NO GOOD NFL MATCH?

Make a custom player

Click his row on Players, then Madden Tools › Create or edit a custom player. A panel opens on the right. Pick one:

  • B – Edited clone. Easy and fast. Start from his clone and change only what's different, like a little slower or two inches shorter.
  • C – From scratch. More involved. No clone needed: every number starts at the average NFL player at his position.

Height, weight and skin tone come first, then the ratings that matter most for his position; the rest are folded under Other ratings. Press Save any time. Anything you don't change stays at the default.

Every player gets a Player type on Players (column P): A exact clone, B edited clone, C from scratch.

Learn more: how his overall is worked out

The panel shows Overall about … as you type. It uses EA's own overall formula for each position, worked out from EA's rosters (it matches every EA player to within 1 point).

For B, his clone's overall moves up or down by however much your changes move it. For C, it's worked out from all his numbers.

His Clone check then reads OK: … (B, edited clone) or OK: … (C, from scratch), and the Roster Kit builds him with your numbers.

Learn more: the switches

Players tab (top right): Show B and Show C hide or show those players on the list. Nothing else changes.

Build Your Squads (row 2): Allow B and Allow C. Off means those players are left out: not counted, not on the Rosters cards, and not built. The replaced team fills their spots.

Custom Players: the Show dropdown at the top shows only the ratings that matter for one group, like QBs or kickers.

Learn more: sharing him (Community Pool)

Madden Tools › Submit a player to the Community Pool emails the player on your selected row to Malcolm. Once he approves him, anyone can find him under Madden Tools › Add a player from the Community Pool and add him to their own copy with one click.

STEP 3 · BUILD YOUR SQUADS TAB

Make a squad

  1. At the top, set Number of squads (1 to 32).
  2. On a squad's row, type its name.
  3. Under Replaces, pick a reserve slot such as Broncos A.
  4. If you like, type the city and nickname the game will show.
Learn more: what "Replaces" means

Your squad takes over one team slot in the game. Pick a reserve slot (Broncos A, Raiders B, Admirals A …) and every real NFL team stays in the game. Your squad wears that team's uniforms, logo and stadium.

In the game's team select, your squad shows up under the team it borrows from (Broncos A is under BRONCOS), with your nickname.

You can pick a real team instead. It turns red, and you're asked first, because that team won't be in the roster you build.

Two squads can't take the same team. The sheet turns dark red and won't allow it.

Learn more: renaming a squad

Type the new name over the old one. Every player on that squad moves to the new name automatically. The squad's chip color goes grey; Step 5 shows how to color it again.

No commas in squad names.

STEP 4 · PLAYERS TAB, COLUMN M

Put players on squads

Click a player's Squads cell (column M) and pick every squad he plays on. One player can be on several squads, and he gets a copy on each.

Learn more: the chips

Each squad shows as a colored chip. Once a player has a squad, his Squads cell turns white so the chips are easy to read. The rest of his row keeps its color.

STEP 5 · OPTIONAL

Choose your colors

Rows: in Settings, color each "made their bones" place's cell the way you want its rows to look. Click back onto Players and the rows update by themselves.
Squad chips: click any Squads cell › Data › Data validation › the rule › pick a color next to the squad › Done.

Learn more: row colors, step by step
  1. Open Settings. Find the list under Where they made their bones.
  2. Replace a name with one of your own places, if you like (Hometown, Little League, High School …).
  3. Click that name's cell. Use the toolbar's Fill color and Text color buttons.
  4. Click back onto Players. Every player from that place now has a row that looks the same.

A plain white cell means no row color.

Learn more: why a renamed squad's chip goes grey

Google keeps each chip color next to the squad's exact name. A new name starts grey. Grey is easy to read, so coloring it again is up to you.

STEP 6 · ROSTERS TAB

Check that your squads are ready

Each squad has a card with its players and a colored bar. The same color shows in the Status column on Build Your Squads.

ColorWhat it means
GreenReady: full roster.
BlueReady: your players only. The other roster spots stay empty.
OrangeWill build. Positions you haven't filled get the replaced team's best player.
GreyEmpty. Skipped when building.
RedFix this first (over 55 players, no team picked, or two squads with the same name or team).
Learn more: the 26 required spots

A squad needs 26 players at set positions to field a team: for example 1 QB, 3 WR, 2 DT, 3 CB, a kicker and a punter (Settings has the full list). If you're short at a position, the builder keeps the best player the replaced team has there. A squad can hold up to 55.

Learn more: Matchup tab

Pick two squads to compare them position by position: what each needs, what each has, and each ready roster side by side.

STEP 7 · ON YOUR PC

Build the roster

  1. In the workbook: File › Download › Microsoft Excel (.xlsx).
  2. Drag the downloaded file onto C:\MaddenRosterKit\BUILD_ROSTER.bat.
  3. Type a short name: letters and numbers, 8 or fewer, no spaces (like GAMEDAY1).
  4. Wait for Done. It takes about a minute.
Learn more: where the files go

The roster is copied straight into Documents\Madden NFL 08\Rosters, ready to load. A copy and a report stay in the kit's output folder. The report lists every squad, who was added, and anything to fix.

If a roster with the same name is already in the Rosters folder, it's replaced. Use a new name to keep older ones.

Learn more: if it says "Nothing was built"

Read the lines above that message. Each one names the squad or player and what to change. The common ones:

  • Pick the team it replaces: a squad with players has no team under Replaces.
  • Both replace the same team: give one of them another team.
  • Over the 55 limit: take some players off that squad.
  • Clone plays a different position: fix the Position or the Clone on that row.
  • Could not find FF-299team-reserves.ros: the kit folder is incomplete. Unzip the full kit again.

Fix them in the workbook, download again, and build again.

STEP 8 · IN THE GAME

Play it

  1. Start Madden 08.
  2. Main Menu › Menu › Files › Load, and pick your roster name.
  3. In team select, find your squad under the team it borrows from.
Learn more: if the game quits before the coin toss

The most common cause is a PC whose Madden mod doesn't match the kit's. Load one of the kit's tested rosters first; if that also quits, the mod is different. Ask the person who sent you the kit.
