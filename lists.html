LIST 1

Master Madden List

264players
155ready to play (clone picked)

Version 1 of the Master List (Oct '26). This list has teammates and friends of mine from Little League through high school and my 1990 season at Glassboro State College. I also included Madison legends and standout players from the 70s to today. You will also find standout players from NJ who played around the late 80s and early 90s. This list is ongoing and will be added to over time. Every player on this list was created by cloning a Madden Great – everyone is good.

The goal of this project is to enjoy playing with created players from the past. It is meant to honor the many players we looked up to as kids and cheered for as adults. We all love ball, and this is a celebration of ball.

Feel free to add, remove, edit, create and organize your own copy of this list as you see fit. Enjoy!

SHARE YOURS

Add players or make your list public

Use the submit form: send your players to the Master Madden List, or make your own list public (at least 22 players, a title and a description). An automatic check runs every hour and emails you the result.

House rules: no offensive, sexual or hateful names or words; no private details about real people; no spam. Lists reported by 3 people are taken down.

AFTER YOU COPY

Three quick things

  1. Rename your copy: [Your name]'s Madden List.
  2. Open READ ME FIRST in the workbook. It explains every tab.
  3. The first time you use the Madden Tools menu, Google asks you to allow it. That is the workbook's own script.
